Alberto Maria Metelli

Tenure-track Assistant Professor · Politecnico di Milano

I am a tenure-track assistant professor of Computer Engineering with the Department of Electronics, Information and Bioengineering (DEIB) and member of the Artificial Intelligence and Robotics Lab (AIRLab) at Politecnico di Milano. My research focuses on the theoretical and algorithmic foundations of sequential decision-making. I am affiliated with the ELLIS Unit Milan.
