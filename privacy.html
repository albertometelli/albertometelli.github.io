Privacy & cookies

Privacy information

This is a static academic website. It does not use advertising, analytics, user accounts, contact forms, or first-party profiling cookies.

Local preferences

The selected light or dark theme is stored in the browser through localStorage. This preference remains on the visitor’s device and is not transmitted to the website owner.

Google Maps

The Contact page includes an optional Google Maps embed. The interactive map is loaded only after the visitor selects “Load interactive map”. Loading it connects to Google, which may receive technical information and set third-party cookies according to its own policies. The address and a direct Google Maps link remain available without loading the embed.

External services

Links to Google Scholar, GitHub, LinkedIn, X, DBLP, institutional websites, publishers, and project websites open external services governed by their respective privacy policies.

Email and hosting

Email links open the visitor’s configured email application; no message is sent through this website. The hosting provider may process standard server logs, such as IP address, requested page, timestamp, and browser information, for security and service operation.
